About me

I’m Theo, a Brazilian-Italian solo developer: I design, build and run every ChewPacaStudios app myself. I studied Life Sciences and Computer Science at McGill University in Montreal, then did a master’s in Bioinformatics at KU Leuven. Now I build apps that listen to music and help people learn to play it.

  • 1

    Your real instrument. No controller, no tapping: you play the harmonica, recorder or flute you already own.

  • 2

    The phone listens. Pitch detection runs on the device and judges each note as you play it.

  • 3

    A path of songs. Short levels unlock songs one step at a time, with practice speeds and a memory mode.
